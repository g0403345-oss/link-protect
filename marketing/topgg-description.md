# 🛡️ Link Protect

Keep your server clean, safe, and on-topic. Link Protect detects and removes
unwanted or dangerous links in real time — its **Scam Shield** stops hijacked
accounts before they can flood your channels, the **Verification Gate** keeps
scam bots from ever getting in, and the **Emergency Lockdown** freezes a raid
with one command. Backed by a full web dashboard and a native iOS app, so you
can manage everything from anywhere.

**Trusted by 10,000+ servers.**

## ⚡ Set Up in 30 Seconds
Run **`/setup-preset`** and pick a protection level — **Minimal, Balanced or
Strict**. One command configures every security shield to a sensible level.
Fine-tune everything later on the dashboard, where a **Security Score (0–100)**
shows exactly how locked-down your server is and what to improve.

## Link & Content Filtering — 16 Shields
Real-time detection and instant deletion of:
- Phishing & malware links (live threat database + Google Safe Browsing)
- Fake Discord Nitro scams and look-alike domains (homoglyph detection)
- Discord server invites
- NSFW / adult sites
- URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl and similar) — **redirects are followed**, so
  shorteners can't hide their real target
- YouTube, Twitch, Google, Steam and GIF links
- Any domain you add to your custom blacklist
- Or block every link at once with a single toggle

## 🛡️ Scam Shield
You've seen it: a hijacked account pastes the same crypto giveaway or fake Nitro
scam into every single channel within seconds. Scam Shield ends that.

- **Cross-channel spam detection** — the same message (link, image or wall of
  text) hitting several channels in seconds triggers instantly: every copy is
  deleted and the account is timed out, kicked or banned. Your choice.
- **Network intelligence** — accounts caught scam-spamming anywhere in the
  Link Protect network are flagged. Enable the **join check** and known scam
  accounts are removed the moment they join your server, before they can post
  a single message.
- **Fair by design** — flags come only from live behaviour, never from keywords
  or reports. Affected users are told why and can appeal at link-protect.com,
  where a human reviews every case.

## ✅ Verification Gate — NEW
Stop scam bots at the door. New members verify on **your own verification page**
(link-protect.com/verify/your-server) with one Discord click — a hurdle bots
can't take.

- **One-click auto-setup** — creates the quarantine role, hides every channel
  from unverified members and adds a #verify info channel. Zero manual work.
- **Your page, your brand** — custom headline, message, accent color and even
  your own background image.
- **Minimum account age** — block freshly created throwaway accounts.

## 🚨 Emergency Lockdown — NEW
Raid in progress? **`/lockdown`** freezes the whole server in seconds: slowmode
on every channel, invites paused, every link blocked. **`/unlock`** restores
everything exactly as it was. Also one click away in the dashboard and the iOS
app — even straight from a push notification.

## Smart Moderation
- Configurable warnings with automatic **kick, ban, or timeout** thresholds
- **Warning decay** — old warnings expire on their own, so a single past mistake
  doesn't count forever
- **Silent mode** — delete links quietly and notify the user by DM, with no
  public callout
- Review, edit and reset any member's warnings at any time

## Fine-Grained Control
- Exempt specific users, roles, channels or entire categories
- **Per-channel rules** — give individual channels their own behaviour
- Custom blacklist & whitelist for full control over what's allowed

## Logging & Insights
- A dedicated log channel for every warning, kick and ban — and you choose
  exactly which events appear in it
- A live activity feed plus statistics & trend charts with Scam Shield and
  raid markers
- A public changelog at link-protect.com/update — see exactly what's new

## Manage From Anywhere
- **Web dashboard** at link-protect.com — modern, intuitive, no commands required
- **iOS app** for iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch — full control on the go, with home
  & lock-screen widgets and push notifications the moment something happens.
  Ban a caught scammer straight from the notification.
- **Team Access** — delegate dashboard control to specific people without giving
  them Discord admin
- **Developer platform** — read-only REST API, signed webhooks for moderation
  events, and live SVG embeds (protection badge, stats card) for your website

## Getting Started
1. Invite Link Protect and let it manage messages in the channels you want protected
2. Run `/setup-preset` — or open the dashboard / iOS app to fine-tune everything
3. Optionally pick a log channel and enable the Verification Gate

**Permissions:** Link Protect needs *Manage Messages* to delete links, plus
*Kick*/*Ban Members* for automatic sanctions and Scam Shield. The Verification
Gate and Lockdown additionally use *Manage Roles*, *Manage Channels* and
*Manage Server* — the dashboard shows you a live permission check. Unsure? Try
it on a test server first — this bot is built to protect, never to disrupt
your server.
